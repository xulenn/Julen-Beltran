    06 Portfolio case study

    A $100,000 portfolio that beat the market.

    As a member of the Finance Student Association at Columbus State, I managed a simulated portfolio from 12 January to 10 April 2026 — equities plus short-term options, built around volatility, macro events and momentum.

    +6.75%

    Best and worst trades

    What the numbers say

    • Return came with risk. A beta of 2.11 and a 26.4% volatility (about 1.7× the S&P's 15.3%) mean this was an aggressive book, not a calm one.
    • Still positive after adjusting for risk. A Sharpe ratio of 1.01 against −0.87 for the index, over a stretch where the S&P 500 fell.
    • Concentration and options cut both ways. The best trade was a long XOM put (+158.8%); the worst were two long calls that lost 99% and 82%. Three equity holdings, 88% in financials — fine for a simulation, and a good lesson in position sizing.
